IMPORTANT SCOPE AND EVALUATION NOTES - The LTspice four-inverter bank
and the physical SN74HC04N experiment are related test arrangements, not
exact replicas. - Hamming distance is treated as a switching-activity
indicator, not as a direct measurement of current or electromagnetic
leakage. - The simplified two-conductor model uses stated geometric
assumptions; it is not a full 3D electromagnetic solver. - The
derivative dB/dt is not itself an electric-field measurement. - HackRF
IQ amplitudes are not calibrated to absolute magnetic flux density. The
comparison therefore focuses on frequency-domain characteristics and
relative spectral features. - For captures made with a 40 MHz centre
frequency and 20 MS/s complex sampling, the nominal RF observation band
is approximately 30–50 MHz. Comparisons should be restricted to the
frequency range actually observed by the recordings.
